**Study:** iTapon, an AI waste-sorting app: pilot and user acceptance test

**Adviser:** ______________________ **Contact:** ______________________

**Purpose.** We are testing whether iTapon helps people put waste in the right bin, and how
easy it is to use.

**What you will do (about 20 minutes).** Try a few short tasks with the app (for example,
scan an item and put it in a bin), then answer a questionnaire. We will time the tasks and
take notes. **We will not record video or audio of you.**

**What we collect.** Your answers, task times and notes, under a code (e.g. P07), never your
name. If you use your own phone, the app works without an account. Scan records (item,
bin, time) are stored as described in the app's privacy notice (itapon.vercel.app/privacy).
Photos are shared only if you tick the photo-sharing box in the app, and you don't need to.

**Voluntary.** You may skip any task or question and stop at any time without giving a reason.
This will not affect your grades, work or any service.

**Risks and benefits.** No known risks beyond normal phone use. Handle the test items
(including a battery) normally; they are clean. There's no direct benefit to you, apart from
learning where waste goes.

**Confidentiality (RA 10173, Data Privacy Act of 2012).** Data is used only for this study and
its defense, stored in the team's private drive, reported only as group results or anonymous
quotes, and deleted __ months after the defense. You may ask us to see or delete your data
by giving your participant code to the contact above.

**PWD participants.** We will read this form aloud on request and you may use your own
assistive settings or a companion.
